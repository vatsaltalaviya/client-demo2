A Holistic Approach to Health

Dr. Sonal Shrivastava is a renowned Endocrinologist specializing in Diabetes, Thyroid, Obesity, PCOS, Infertility, and general Hormone health. Based in Indore, India , she is the visionary Founder & Director of Sovin Hormone Clinic .

Attached to CHL Hospitals , one of Indore's best medical institutions, Dr. Shrivastava is also the founder of Hormone Health Awareness , an initiative dedicated to public education and empowerment.

Integrative Endocrinology: Treating the Whole Person

Dr. Sonal is the pioneer of Integrative Endocrinology in India , and among the very few such specialists globally. This advanced philosophy adds scientifically proven modalities to conventional treatments, focusing on the patient as a whole —not just a disease or system.

Her philosophy: "To live an optimal life, each individual must be balanced at all three levels: body, mind, and soul ."

For more insight, visit Hormone Health Awareness on Facebook or YouTube channel Dr Sonal Endo.

Exceptional Qualifications & Expertise

Dr. Sonal Shrivastava holds the distinction of being the only qualified female Endocrinologist (Hormone Specialist) in Central India to date, a testament to her dedication and academic excellence.

Academic Distinction

  • NTSE Scholar and school topper throughout her early education.
  • Selected in the first attempt in MP-PMT (1994) , achieving 15th rank across the state.
  • Received Merit-Scholarship throughout MBBS at MGM Medical College, Indore (1999).

Specialized Training

  • MD in Internal Medicine (2004) from Government Medical College Baroda, MS University, Baroda, Gujarat.
  • Super-speciality in Endocrinology from Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, Cochin, Kerala.
  • Further training at the Department of Endocrinology, Post Graduate Institute, Chandigarh .

Global Certification & Memberships

Dr. Shrivastava is a Diplomate National Board in Endocrinology and a Member of the National Academy of Medical Sciences .

She cleared the Speciality Certification Exam of Endocrinology by the Royal College of Physicians, UK , making her one of the very few UK Certified Endocrinologists of India .

She is an active member of several prestigious medical societies, including the Endocrine Society, USA , the Indian Thyroid Society, Endocrine Society of India, and the Association of Physicians of India.

As a National Speaker at various CMEs and conferences, and through extensive community service and awareness camps, she continues to shape the future of endocrine health in India.
