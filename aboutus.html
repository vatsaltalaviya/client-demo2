About Us

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Sonal Shrivastava

Dr. Sonal Shrivastava is a renowned Endocrinologist specializing in Diabetes, Thyroid, Obesity, PCOS, Infertility & Hormone disorders. She is based in Indore, India and is the Founder & Director of Sovin Hormone Clinic.

She is also attached to CHL Hospitals, one of the best hospitals in Indore. Dr. Sonal is the founder of "Hormone Health Awareness", an initiative dedicated to public education.

"Integrative Endocrinology adds scientifically proven modalities to conventional treatment. To live an optimal life, each individual must be balanced at all three levels: body, mind, and soul."

She is the pioneer of Integrative Endocrinology in India and is among the very few specialists worldwide to practice this holistic approach, treating the patient as a whole rather than just a disease.

For more info, visit Hormone Health Awareness on Facebook or her YouTube channel Dr Sonal Endo.

Education
  • MBBS (1999) - MGM Medical College, Indore (Merit-Scholarship throughout).
  • MD in Internal Medicine (2004) - Govt. Medical College Baroda, MS University, Gujarat (Selected via All-India Pre-PG merit).
  • Super-speciality in Endocrinology - Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences (AIMS), Cochin.
  • Further training at the Department of Endocrinology, PGIMER, Chandigarh.
Certifications
  • Diplomate National Board (DNB) in Endocrinology.
  • Speciality Certificate in Endocrinology (UK) - Royal College of Physicians.
  • One of the very few UK-certified Endocrinologists in India.
  • Only qualified female Endocrinologist (Hormone Specialist) in Central India to date.
Honours & Memberships
